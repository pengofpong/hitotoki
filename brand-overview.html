Brand overview

Purpose, positioning, persona, and voice, following the opening chapter of the book.

Purpose & mission

Purpose: Provide customers the resources to create quality bonding time with anyone.

Mission: “To bring people together.”

Brand pillars

Product & Services

Original board games; membership; in-store play and take-home purchases.

Substance

Togetherness, strategic thinking, community; generational relationships and offline experiences.

Positioning

Young adults and families; lighthearted fun in spirit and competitive/challenging in mind.

Differentiators

All-employee involvement and intentionality of time and space.

Brand persona

Inviting

Welcoming and accessible.

Refined

Considered and understated.

Imaginative

Creativity through meaningful experiences.

Thoughtful

Attention to people, time, and connection.

Brand voice

Conversational, warm, considered, inviting; casual, familiar, understated/implied.

Tagline: “Our time, offline”

Avoid: aggression, excessive technical terms, formal/corporate language, exclusivity, childishness, and overly energetic expression.
