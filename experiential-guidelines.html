The book’s grid, pacing, and examples of the visual system in use.

Layout system

Print reference: 9 × 6 inches; 6 columns; 7 rows; 0.125-inch gutters. The web layout uses a comparable grid based on a single page rather than a spread.

  • Layouts should reflect togetherness and wholeness.
  • Encourage readers to slow down.
  • Leave breathing room on all sides.
  • Avoid rulers as dividers; color blocking is acceptable.
  • Use sun and moon secondary icons only for section headers.
