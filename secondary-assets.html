Secondary visual assets

Construction rules, secondary symbols, and controlled variations.

Lockup grid & secondary symbols

Junihoshi is a twelve-pointed starburst with rounded corners. Its central circular area accommodates interchangeable pictograms. Preserve the approved symbol geometry and proportions.

Creating variations

Allowed

Simple, solid-filled pictograms fitting entirely within the inner circle, with a punch-out effect. New variations require approval.

Prohibited

Horizontal logos, effects added to supplied logo files, tilted or reoriented assets, unapproved color combinations.

Store-only

The sakura symbol and Sakura Pink are reserved for specified store-related applications.

Approved motifs

Die, star, crescent, rook, and circle are interchangeable decorative symbols.
